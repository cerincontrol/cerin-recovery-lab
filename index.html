CERIN Recovery Lab is a controlled, nonproduction workflow for testing whether an encrypted, synthetic research-journal backup can be retrieved and cryptographically verified after loss of its original computer.

The laboratory

How it works

The laboratory separates the creation of a backup from independent verification of its contents.

01

Generate synthetic evidence

Create a small test journal, signed checkpoint, checksums and a publicly verifiable trust anchor. No production research data is needed.

02

Store encrypted test files

Use rclone with Google Drive to hold an isolated laboratory copy protected by a separate encryption key.

03

Verify on a separate host

Download with independently safeguarded read-only credentials and validate the exact recovered bytes and journal signature.

Scope and limitations

Laboratory testing, not production recovery

A successful test verifies a specific synthetic bundle. It does not establish complete recovery of production databases, continuous acquisition history, Google-account resilience or authorization to replay, delete or prune backups.

LABNonproduction

Access and privacy

Operator-controlled Google Drive access

Google OAuth authorization is used only by an operator-configured rclone client. Upload and recovery connections can request different Google Drive permissions. The public website itself has no Google sign-in function and does not ask visitors for credentials.

Credentials, encryption secrets, backup contents and test results must not be published in this site's source code. The privacy policy explains the intended data handling and how to revoke access.
